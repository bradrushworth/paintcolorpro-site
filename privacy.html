Privacy Policy

Paint Color Pro · Last updated 6 September 2026

Paint Color Pro works entirely on your device, and your data stays on your device. This policy explains the little it does with data, in plain language.

What the app stores

  • Favourites — colours you mark as favourites are saved in a local database on your device only.
  • Projects — the rooms and exteriors you set up, the colours you assign to their surfaces, and the schemes you save are kept in the same local database.
  • Taste training — your likes and dislikes on the colour schemes shown during training and in the scheme feed are stored locally and used only to rank suggestions for you. You can reset them at any time from Settings.
  • Settings — preferences such as Pro mode are stored locally.
  • Photos — when you use photo matching, the photo is processed entirely on your device to extract its colours. Photos are not uploaded, stored by the app, or shared.

None of this is ever uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What leaves your device

Nothing. The app makes no network requests. The paint charts and colour schemes it uses are bundled inside the app itself.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • No collection of personal information of any kind.

Permissions

  • Camera / photo library — used only when you choose to match colours from a photo, only at the moment you use the feature.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.
