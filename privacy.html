Privacy Policy

Paint Color Pro · Last updated 22 August 2026

Paint Color Pro is designed to work on your device, with your data staying on your device. This policy explains the little it does with data, in plain language.

What the app stores

  • Favourites — colours you mark as favourites are saved in a local database on your device only. They are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Photos — when you use photo matching, the photo is processed entirely on your device to extract its colours. Photos are not uploaded, stored by the app, or shared.

What leaves your device

  • The optional "Inspire me" feature sends only anonymous colour values (numbers like RGB 255, 0, 0) to the Colormind palette service (colormind.io) to generate palette suggestions. No personal information, photos, or identifiers are sent.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • No collection of personal information of any kind.

Permissions

  • Camera / photo library — used only when you choose to match colours from a photo, only at the moment you use the feature.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.
