New in 2.3: colour schemes for any hero colour, projects with surfaces, taste training, and the full Taubmans, British Paints and Colorbond ranges.

Schemes for the colour you chose

Start from a colour you already love. The app builds walls, trim, ceiling and feature schemes around it, ranks them, and tells you in plain language why each one works. Every suggestion is a real tin you can buy.

Projects that remember your decisions

Set up a room or an exterior, lock the surfaces you have decided, and let the rest follow. A fixed brick, roof or Colorbond colour shapes what gets suggested.

Suggestions that learn your taste

A short swipe-through of real schemes teaches the app what you like — warm or cool, tonal or bold — and ranks suggestions your way. Everything stays on your phone.

Real brand charts, offline

Around 13,900 colours from Dulux, Taubmans, British Paints and Colorbond, bundled on-device. Browse, search and filter by hue with no network at all.

Match from a photo

Snap a wall, a fabric, a sunset. The app extracts its palette and finds the closest real paints for every swatch, and lets you take any of them into a project.

Colour science that's honest

Matching uses CIEDE2000, the industry-standard perceptual colour difference. Light reflectance and WCAG contrast are shown for every colour, scheme reasons name the undertones at play, and Pro mode shows the numbers behind them.
